# Knot 1.1.107

## Highlights

- Sharing no longer silences your own desktop. The capture loopback is pinned
  to your real speakers, muted, then faded up after apps move onto the share
  sink.
- Knot’s own playback (voice and the friend’s share sound) stays on your
  speakers instead of being swept into the capture sink.
- Incoming share audio starts muted so Chromium allows autoplay, then unmutes
  and fades in so you can hear the friend’s computer sound without a click.
- Stopping a screen share no longer kills the voice call. Computer sound
  leaves the reserved screen m-line before capture stops, and the microphone
  is reattached even if renegotiation fails.
- Leaving a call and rejoining works on the existing peer connection. Hangup
  parks a live silent track instead of ending the friend’s receiver, and Start
  voice no longer treats a brief ICE disconnect as a dead call.
