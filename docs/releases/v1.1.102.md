# Knot 1.1.102

## Highlights

- A friend’s incoming screen share no longer opens as a white rectangle.
  Empty video stays dark until a real picture exists, and native AV1 draws on
  a canvas instead of Chromium’s white generated-track frame.
- Starting a share no longer blasts a piercing tone through your speakers.
  The Linux desktop-audio return path starts muted and fades up after the
  capture sink has settled. PCM is clamped so a spike cannot hit full scale.
- Computer sound stays attached across Stop/Share and group native shares.
  The reserved audio m-line is rebound instead of torn down, and a hung
  PipeWire capture is retried instead of going silent.
