# Knot 1.1.112

## Highlights

- Incoming friend share audio plays on the media element at audible volume, not
  a muted hidden player. Diagnostics show on the stream badge (the call log is
  hidden in the UI).
- Native AV1 encoder ceiling is 200 Mbps so a live 4K share does not fill the
  data channel and go black after a short run.
