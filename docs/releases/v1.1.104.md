# Knot 1.1.104

## Highlights

- Stopping a screen share no longer kills the voice call. The computer-sound
  lane cannot replace the microphone track with silence, and the mic is
  reattached when the share ends.
- Leaving a call no longer requires restarting Knot. Hangup finishes before
  a new call can start, and Start voice works whenever the peer connection
  is still open.
- Incoming shares stay off Chromium’s white empty video without hiding the
  decoder. The picture is blitted onto a dark canvas.
- Linux share audio no longer blasts the speakers if the loopback cannot be
  muted, and desktop apps still move onto the capture sink.
