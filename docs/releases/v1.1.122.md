# Knot 1.1.122

## Unstable connections

- **Calls come back after the network drops.** Knot used to wait about 30
  seconds before trying to reconnect a call, and the reconnect itself never
  really restarted the connection. It now reconnects a few seconds into a drop,
  keeps trying while the network is down, and in testing was talking again
  within a second of the network returning.
- **Screen sharing on a slow link no longer loops.** A share whose first picture
  arrived slowly was restarted over and over (the "pick a screen" window kept
  coming back) and viewers saw black. Viewers now wait for the picture.
- **Share changes reach your friend even when one connection has died.** Start,
  stop and quality changes used to be lost if the setup connection had dropped,
  which left the viewer on a black screen.
- **Direct messages are never lost.** You can type and send while Knot is
  offline. A message shows "Waiting for connection" or "Sending…" until Knot's
  server confirms it, and is sent again after a reconnect or a restart. A
  message the server refuses is marked "Not delivered".
- A connection that silently stopped answering is replaced within seconds
  instead of after a minute.
- Fixed Knot asking you to sign in again, and no longer reconnecting, when
  something was sent at the moment it reconnected.
- **Updates download on a bad connection.** Update checks retry, a dropped
  download continues where it stopped, and a backup update address is used
  when the main one is unreachable.

## Microphone

- **Noise suppression no longer silences your microphone for the rest of the
  call.** The RNNoise filter stopped for good if its input was interrupted for
  even a moment.
- **Knot picks your microphone up again when it disappears.** Unplugging a USB
  headset, a Bluetooth headset reconnecting or the sound system restarting used
  to leave you silent until you rejoined. Knot now switches to the microphone
  again (or to the system default if yours is gone), in calls and in voice
  channels, and keeps you muted if you were.
- Changing the microphone, echo cancellation or noise suppression during a call
  now applies straight away instead of on the next call.

## NVIDIA GPU video decoding on Linux

- **NVIDIA users decode shared screens on the GPU without installing anything.**
  Knot now includes its own nvidia-vaapi-driver, built from a newer version than
  any release. The newest release (0.0.18) decodes NVIDIA-encoded AV1 shares
  with the wrong size, so the picture smeared after every key frame.
- Knot checks GPU decoding at every launch by decoding a short clip on the GPU
  and on the CPU and comparing the pictures. If they differ, Knot remembers that
  for this driver and restarts on CPU decoding, so a broken setup can never show
  you a garbled or white share. Updating your NVIDIA driver checks again.
- GeForce RTX 20 and GTX 16 cards, which cannot decode AV1, are now checked with
  H.264 and keep GPU decoding for H.264 and VP9 shares.
- **Settings → Screen sharing** explains why shares decode on your CPU when the
  NVIDIA decoder is off.

## Other fixes

- A Linux update no longer closes Knot for good when Knot is installed in a
  folder your account cannot change (such as /opt). Knot says so before
  downloading, and starts the old version again if the swap fails.
- Restarting Knot from inside the app works in the AppImage.
- Fixed an error at every startup that skipped part of the call cleanup.
