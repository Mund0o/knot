# Knot 1.1.126

## Watch Together

- **YouTube links play inside Knot again.** The player showed "Video player
  configuration error (Error 153)" because YouTube refuses an embed that arrives
  without a Referer, and Knot's page never sends one. The player's request now
  carries one.

## Calls and screen sharing

- **Clean microphone icons.** The mute buttons showed a music-note character. They
  now show a plain microphone while you are live and the same microphone crossed
  out in red while you are muted, and the icon switches as you toggle.
- **A clearer screen share button.** A monitor with an up arrow while you are not
  sharing, and a monitor with a stop square while you are.
- **You no longer hear a screen share you are not watching.** Stopping a share left
  its sound playing through a second route, moving the stream volume slider
  unmuted it, and in a server room the sound unmuted the moment a friend's share
  started, even if you had not opened it. Sound now plays only for the share you
  are watching.

## File transfers

- **Files go over the encrypted direct connection only.** Knot used to try a TCP
  lane first, which needs a forwarded router port, opened a listening port on
  your PC, and made the first transfer wait on it. That is gone. The TCP choices,
  the TCP port setting and the port-forwarding instructions are removed from
  Settings, and a TCP offer from an older Knot is refused at once so the other
  side carries on without waiting.
