# Knot 1.1.109

## Highlights

- Share computer sound is captured and returned to the speakers. The loopback
  is found by its PipeWire module id, moved onto the real sink, and no longer
  torn down when Pulse reports the sink as a number.
- Native 4K60 AV1 no longer drops a whole GOP because one key took longer than
  260 ms to encode and send. LAN shares keep sending frames; the decoder is
  not rebuilt on a dark-but-live picture.
