# Knot 1.1.101

## Highlights

- 4K screen share no longer hitchs every GOP. Keys start as soon as one 60 KB
  part fits instead of waiting for the whole IDR, and the pump jumps to the
  latest picture instead of sending old GOPs in order. Live latency stays at
  about 110 ms and never past 260 ms. Encode bitrate is not cut to paper over
  a slow path.
- Computer sound retries a silent PipeWire start instead of giving up after
  one race, and share tiles stay dark instead of flashing white when you
  come back to the window.
- After Knot measures your connection, the bitrate slider only goes as high
  as that path can carry. A 40 Mbps link offers about 30 Mbps, not 250.
