# Knot 1.1.96

## Highlights

- Caps a live share to the slowest person in the call. A gigabit sender with a
  50 Mbps friend stays around 37 Mbps, then can rise if that friend advertises
  a faster path. Same-Wi-Fi shares are not clipped by a slow WAN speed test.
- Aborts the Cloudflare speed probe when you go live, so the test cannot starve
  a slow friend. Watching a share no longer writes a low download sample into
  the six-hour cache.
- Stops a healthy WebRTC share from ratcheting toward zero just because the
  encoder is at its cap, and stops one congested viewer from cutting everyone
  else.
- A decoder failure or GPU restart on one native AV1 viewer no longer recaptures
  the whole room as H.264. Group native queues keep the init segment and latest
  key instead of dropping recovery frames.
- Settings, profile photo, and account keys keep surviving app updates.
