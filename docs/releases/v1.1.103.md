# Knot 1.1.103

## Highlights

- Voice no longer dies around ten minutes in. ICE restarts instead of hanging
  up when TURN or NAT drops, and the call heartbeat no longer rebuilds a
  silent playback graph on both sides.
- A friend’s incoming share stays dark until a real picture exists, then
  draws on canvas. Chromium’s empty white video is never uncovered.
- Linux-to-Linux computer sound actually reaches the capture sink. Firefox
  and games are no longer skipped as “modules,” and the viewer plays after
  speaker selection.
