# Knot 1.1.108

## Highlights

- Sharing no longer blasts the sharer's speakers. The capture loopback parks
  on a muted dummy sink first, then moves to the real speakers and fades up.
- Friend computer sound is not dropped during a busy 4K AV1 encode.
- Knot's own playback (voice and the incoming share) is left on the real
  speakers instead of being swept into the capture sink as Chromium/chrome.
