# Knot 1.1.124

## Watching a friend's screen share

- **A share that cannot get its first picture through no longer hangs.** On a
  slow or lossy connection the sharer's first AV1 picture could never arrive,
  and the viewer sat on "loading slowly" forever, with no frames and no way out.
  If no picture has arrived 12 seconds after a share starts, Knot now switches
  that share to the bandwidth-capped compatibility codec by itself.
