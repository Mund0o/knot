# Knot 1.1.125

## Watching a friend's screen share

- **A struggling connection buffers instead of lowering the quality.** Knot
  never switches a share to another codec or a smaller picture because the
  connection is weak. 1.1.124 did that after 12 seconds; it is gone. The share
  stays AV1 at the resolution your friend picked.
- **A ring of running cats shows when a share is buffering.** The last picture
  that arrived stays on screen underneath, and the cats disappear as soon as
  pictures flow again.
- **A still screen is not mistaken for a stall.** A friend sharing a screen that
  is not changing sends no pictures, so their Knot now sends a small "still
  here" message. The cats only appear when pictures and that message both stop.
  Both of you need 1.1.125 for this.
- On a weak link a WebRTC share now slows down instead of shrinking: the
  resolution is kept and the frame rate drops.
- Knot still switches a share if a viewer's decoder genuinely cannot decode it.
