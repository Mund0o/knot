# Knot 1.1.117

## Fixes

- **A Windows screen share shows and plays on Linux.** The call host was
  dropping the share update, and Linux was asking for AV1, which decoded as a
  blank silent tile. The share is accepted, and Linux prefers VP9. Inbound
  H.264 is constrained baseline so the picture can decode.
- **Fullscreen covers the window.** The share stage leaves the call card and
  fills the screen, with the picture fitted inside it. Volume and Stop
  Watching stay above the picture.
- **Computer audio no longer blasts or sounds robotic.** Playback is one path,
  Opus error correction is off for the share, startup clicks are held back,
  and loud desktop audio is turned down instead of deleted.
