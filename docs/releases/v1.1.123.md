# Knot 1.1.123

## Watching a friend's screen share

- **The share now says what is going on.** Under a friend's share you see the
  picture size, how many frames per second arrive, the bitrate, the codec, and
  whether your GPU or CPU decodes it. If frames arrive slowly, the line says
  your friend's PC or connection is sending few frames. If they arrive fast but
  show slowly, it says this computer is falling behind.
- **Share sound is checked, not assumed.** "Audio received" only meant your
  friend's Knot had attached a sound track. Knot now checks every few seconds
  and shows "sound playing", "no sound arriving from your friend", "sound
  arriving but silent" or "sound muted". If sound arrives but is not playing,
  Knot restarts playback, then switches to a second playback route.
- Share sound no longer depends on the window being redrawn to fade in, so a
  busy or slow screen cannot leave it at volume zero.

## NVIDIA GPU video decoding on Linux

- **Knot notices when your NVIDIA GPU cannot keep up with a real share.** The
  startup check uses a small clip, so a full-size share can still be too much.
  When frames arrive much faster than they decode, Knot says so, stops telling
  sharers you decode that codec on the GPU (so they lower the bitrate), and
  decodes on the CPU after the next restart. It does not restart in the middle
  of a call.
- **Settings → Screen sharing → Decode shared screens on the NVIDIA GPU** turns
  GPU decoding off by hand (restart to apply), without turning off hardware
  acceleration for everything else.
