# Knot 1.1.91

## Highlights

- Adds ten complete themes: Bento, Brutalist, Cozy, Cyberdeck, Minimal, Retro,
  Comic, Library, Studio, and Noir.
- Introduces modular cards, heavy raw borders, soft large controls, clipped
  sci-fi geometry, distraction-free chat, classic desktop bevels, speech
  balloons, book-spine navigation, a horizontal top dock, and cinematic
  monochrome framing.
- Expands the Appearance picker to 24 saved themes with distinctive previews
  and short descriptions.
- Keeps fixed member rosters readable and visually consistent across every new
  light theme.
- Extends UI regression coverage to apply and structurally verify all 24
  themes.
