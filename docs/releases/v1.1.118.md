# Knot 1.1.118

## Fixes

- **Received video is no longer a white screen on NVIDIA Linux.** Knot forced
  VA-API hardware decode on NVIDIA, which Chromium could not import, so every
  shared screen and call video painted white. NVIDIA now decodes in software;
  AMD and Intel are unchanged.
- **Screen audio no longer chokes.** The capture queue holds ~200 ms instead of
  ~60 ms, the playback buffer rides out render stalls, audio at 44.1 kHz is
  resampled continuously instead of per chunk, and loud sound is no longer
  flattened. Viewers also keep a small jitter floor on share audio.
- **Online and offline are accurate.** Friends no longer come back "online"
  from the last session, everyone goes offline when you lose the connection,
  dead connections are dropped after about 80 seconds, and "On this Wi-Fi"
  expires when a friend leaves the network.
- **Push-to-talk keys no longer throw an error**, and a startup error in call
  handling is gone.
- **The YouTube watch bar no longer covers "Voice Connected" or the member
  pictures** in a server voice channel.

## Themes

- Readable text in all eleven light themes (link cards, links, call buttons,
  header pills, the sound toggle), plus a few dark-theme and Pulp fixes.
- Three new layouts: **Pebble** (messenger bubbles), **Drift** (full-bleed chat
  with a hover drawer) and **Marquee** (conversations as a top strip).

## Voice

- Voice quality tiers rise to 64 / 96 / 128 kbps depending on upload speed.
