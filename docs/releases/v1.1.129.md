# Knot 1.1.129

Screen shares and calls from 1.1.128 still work with this version in both
directions; nothing about how they travel changed.

## Screen sharing: telling you what is wrong

- **The line under a friend's share names the cause when fewer pictures are
  shown than arrive.** It used to say "this computer is falling behind" for
  every case. It now says what was measured: that this window is redrawn only
  so many times a second (another program using the graphics card, or the
  window covered), that this computer decodes only so many pictures a second,
  or, with nothing measured, the old general wording.
- **Sound that is not there is no longer reported as "arriving but silent".** A
  share without any sound still sends silent packets, and they were counted as
  sound. The line now says "your friend is not sharing sound" when their sound
  is off or could not start, "sound arriving" for the first seconds, and "your
  friend's sound is silent" when sound was shared but nothing has played for a
  few seconds.
- **A friend who joins or resumes watching a share that has sound is told so
  again.** Until now only the first viewer heard that the share carried sound.

## Sharing sound (Windows and Linux)

- **Sound that cannot start says why.** The person sharing used to see
  "starting sound capture" for the rest of the share while the viewer received
  silence. It now says "sound unavailable" with the reason, and the viewer is
  told there is no sound.
- **Sound capture that runs but hears nothing says so.** After eight seconds
  without anything playing, the sharer's line reads "sound on, but nothing is
  playing on this computer" (Windows), and goes back to "sound live" as soon as
  something plays. Capture that only began hearing sound after the share did is
  now shown as live instead of staying on "waiting for computer sound".

## Behind the scenes

- New tests: a 1080p58 H.264 share through the real player (every picture
  shown, with and without uneven arrival), and the sound lines for sharer and
  viewer in the real page.
- The end-to-end test rig can keep the app's own graphics settings
  (`E2E_GPU=1`); it used to turn the graphics card off in every run.
